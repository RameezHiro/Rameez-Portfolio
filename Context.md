RAMEEZ: THE DEVELOPER'S ARC — Full Chat Summary
1. Core Project

Project: RAMEEZ: THE DEVELOPER'S ARC

Core concept:

An interactive manga about becoming a software engineer.

The manga is the storytelling mechanism, not the product itself.

The portfolio's real purpose is to demonstrate:

Strong software engineering ability
AI/ML knowledge
Backend/system-building ability
Projects and technical evidence
Hackathon experience
Growth and ambition
Personality and problem-solving
North Star

Build an interactive manga-shaped portfolio that tells the story of Rameez becoming a strong software engineer with deep AI knowledge, while making his actual engineering ability impossible to miss.

The desired visitor reaction:

"This guy is going places."

2. Overall Design Direction
Art Style

Manga + Modern Manhwa Hybrid

Characteristics:

Clean manga ink linework
Detailed Manhwa-style composition
Cinematic grayscale
Controlled halftone/screentone
Strong blacks and negative space
Sakura pink only for meaningful moments
No excessive anime exaggeration
Mood

Ambitious, quiet strategist

The protagonist is not portrayed as an exaggerated "AI genius."

He evolves from:

Student → Learner → Builder → Engineer → Current Rameez
